notes/pb-ratio-and-margin-vs-markup.md

Price-to-Book (P/B) Ratio

Compares a company's share price to its book value (what would be left for shareholders if the company sold every asset and paid off every liability).

P/B = Share Price ÷ Book Value per Share

Where it actually matters:

  • Useful for asset-heavy industries: plants, land, heavy machinery, cement, power, infrastructure, gas.
  • Not very useful for asset-light industries: software, consulting, tech, media, web development, where value sits in people and ideas rather than physical book value.

Margin vs. Markup

Worked example: a pencil bought at ₹10 (Cost Price) and sold at ₹15 (Selling Price), a profit of ₹5.

Margin = Profit ÷ Selling Price
Markup = Profit ÷ Cost Price

Read: the same ₹5 profit gives two different percentages depending on which number it's measured against, margin against Selling Price, markup against Cost Price.
